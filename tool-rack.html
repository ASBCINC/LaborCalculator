Watts: use typical draw from the spec sheet (amps at idle draw far less than their rating). Heat: every watt in the rack ends up as about 3.41 BTU/hr. Power: a continuous load should stay under 80% of the circuit. Count shelves, blanks, vents and the power conditioner in RU. Over about 1,500 BTU/hr in a closed closet, plan active ventilation.
