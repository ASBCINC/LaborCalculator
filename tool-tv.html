16:9 screens. Viewing angle is how wide the screen looks from the main seat: about 30° (SMPTE) is the comfortable minimum for a living room, 36–40° (THX) is cinema-like, over 50° is too big for most people. Mounting height puts the screen centre at or under 15° above seated eye level; above a fireplace, plan a pull-down or tilt mount. For conference rooms and anything people need to read, size with AVIXA DISCAS instead.
