Measure each window three times across and three times down. Enter inches as 36 3/8, 36-3/8 or 36.375. The sheet works out the size to order and flags problems. It saves on this device until you start a new sheet.

#RoomWindowMountOrder W × HTypeFabricControlNotes
Inside mount: the size to order is the opening (narrowest width, longest height). Enter the opening and let the factory take its deductions; check in Lutron Designer whether you're entering opening or shade size. Outside mount: widest width plus the overlap on each side, longest height plus the overlap at the top. Widths that differ by more than 1/4" mean the opening is out of square; note it for the installer.
