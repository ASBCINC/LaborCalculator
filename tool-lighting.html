Loads = separately controlled lighting circuits (cans, pendants, under-cabinet, fan, exterior). Dimmable loads get dimmers, the rest get switches. Multi-location loads need a companion (or Pico) at each extra location. A scene keypad is counted at the main entry. Room types are only starting points. Before pricing, check every load's type (LED, ELV, MLV, 0-10 V) and wattage, and check the system's device limits, in Lutron Designer.
